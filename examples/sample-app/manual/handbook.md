# Sample Shop handbook

This is the short handbook of the sample application. It is written the way handbooks of small
business programs usually are: it explains the masks and leaves out most of the rules.

## Customers

The page Customers lists all customers. Press New customer to enter a customer, or select a row
and press Edit customer.

Every customer has a name, a city, a group (A, B or C) and a credit limit. The group is used for
prices. Customers of group A are the best customers.

## Articles

The page Articles lists the articles with price and stock. Press Edit article to change the name,
the price or the stock of the selected article. The tax class of an article cannot be changed.

## Writing an invoice

Press New invoice on the page Invoices. Enter the customer number, then one line per article: the
SKU, the quantity, Add line. The totals are shown below the lines. Save invoice stores the invoice
and gives it a number.

Good customers get a discount on larger invoices. An invoice is refused when the customer would
owe more than the credit limit allows.

## Payments

Select an invoice and press Mark paid when the money has arrived. Paid invoices no longer count
against the credit limit.
